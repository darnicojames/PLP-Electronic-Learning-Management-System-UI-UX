About PLP ELMS

Bridging the gap between traditional education and digital innovation.

Our Identity

The Pamantasan ng Lungsod ng Pasig (PLP) Electronic Learning Management System is a state-of-the-art platform designed to enhance the academic journey of our community.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, high-quality digital education that empowers students with the skills needed for global competitiveness.

Our Vision

To be a leading center of excellence in technology-integrated learning, fostering innovation and academic integrity.

Core Values

🎓

Excellence

Striving for the highest standards in every academic pursuit.

🤝

Integrity

Upholding honesty and ethical conduct in the digital space.

🚀

Innovation

Embracing new technologies to redefine the learning experience.

PLP ELMS

Dedicated to providing quality digital education to the citizens of Pasig City. Empowering the youth through technology and innovation.
